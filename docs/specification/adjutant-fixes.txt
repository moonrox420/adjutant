## The key conclusion

The central security primitives—RLS, credential encryption, SSRF defense, approval signing, gateway validation, campaign-build journaling, remote-stop verification—are markedly stronger than the late-stage scaffolding.

The problem is that **some newer features did not reuse those primitives**.

The most dangerous future mistake would be wiring the current autonomous runner, billing cancellation, or video renderer into user-facing production merely because their unit tests are green. Those three modules currently report realities that their external systems have not established.
